MAVLab turns an Android phone into a drone education simulator.

SIMULATE
Run an offline quadcopter simulator with physics, autopilot, telemetry, battery behavior, missions, and failures. No drone hardware or server is required.

FLY
Use phone tilt controls or manual fallback controls to fly. Watch altitude, speed, attitude, GPS, battery, and mode telemetry update live.

CONNECT TO QGROUNDCONTROL
MAVLab speaks MAVLink over UDP so QGroundControl can connect to the simulated vehicle, upload supported plans and observe telemetry. Protocol support is bounded; MAVLab is not official ArduPilot firmware or a real-aircraft control system.

EXPLORE FOUR VIEWS
Use Cockpit for vehicle state, Control for deliberate flight actions, SIM for the digital twin and received route, and Ops for health, diagnostics, recording and setup. A nine-step introduction helps you begin. Compact tabs, guarded swipes and optional full-screen display keep navigation accessible.

EXPERIMENT
Use Ops to inject and reset supported sensor, wind, motor, battery and payload failures. Upload supported missions, fence regions and rally points from QGroundControl. Enable fencing explicitly to explore engine-driven recovery. Returns use flat ground and straight-line control, not obstacle or terrain avoidance.

RECORD DELIBERATELY
Recording is off by default. Begin a session in Ops or opt into recording on arm while disarmed. Stop/save, review and export your recorded session without a cloud account.

BUILT FOR CLASSROOMS
MAVLab is offline-first, open source, and designed for drone systems education where hardware access is limited.
